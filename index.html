About the practice

Not a technology company.
A research practice that builds things.

Aethics Deacon Nodes was founded by K. Thangamani Prabu — 29 years across mechanical engineering, broadcast television, feature film, photography, voice engineering, and AI systems. No formal training connecting any of them.

The absence of formal boundaries is the point. Research here begins from lived non-technical experience and tests whether that experience can reach back to others in the same position. The goal is not to explain AI — it is to dissolve the access gap entirely.

LOOP SYSTEM
A closed feedback loop for AI interactions — designed for users who cannot evaluate model output technically. Surfaces signal without requiring interpretation, so non-technical adults can course-correct AI outputs themselves.

SPARK ENGINE
Lightweight task orchestration layer that translates natural-language intent into structured AI workflows. Target user: a solo operator with no programming background, running complex multi-step work on a phone.

Status & track record

Self-financed. Built in the open.

Aethics Deacon Nodes is a Udyam-registered micro enterprise (UDYAM-TN-02-0463723), incorporated and operating since 1 April 2026. It has no institutional or equity funding; every project is financed by the founder. That constraint is the reason for the programs listed below, not a gap in the work.

The practice builds on Anthropic's Claude and Claude Code as its primary AI tools. Its code is public at github.com/adnodes-adds: FullMark, a source-to-Markdown converter for video, PDF, images, web and documents; and ui-standards, a standard-parts catalogue for AI-built apps packaged as an Agent Skill. As an early-stage, bootstrapped startup in its first year, it is applying to Claude Startups for Applied AI guidance, higher rate limits and API credits.
